# Content Briefs: 6 Core Money Pages & 30 Topical Cluster Articles

## Standards & Quality Checklist for All Content
- **Immediate Direct Answer:** 250-400 character crisp summary located above the fold directly answering primary user intent.
- **Centerpiece Visual Dominance:** The primary functional tool (calculator, checklist, matrix) or authoritative comparison must be immediately accessible.
- **Nepal-Specific Grounding:** References official authorities (Inland Revenue Department, Office of Company Registrar, ICAN, Labor Act 2074).
- **Zero Hallucination Policy:** Clear disclaimers that management consulting does not replace statutory audit (ICAN) or legal court representation.
- **No Vague Hype:** Avoid terms like "Nepal's #1", "guaranteed wealth", or "100% tax exemption".
